Turn On the Vigil Site

How to put the Vigil portal online when you need it, and take it offline afterwards.

Turn it on

  1. Make sure your laptop is on, connected to the internet, and logged in.
  2. Open this folder: F:\Gitrepo\vigil mdr\tools
  3. Double-click site-on.cmd.
  4. Wait for up to a minute until the black window shows Vigil is ONLINE, followed by an address ending in trycloudflare.com.
  5. Copy that address into your browser.
  6. Sign in with your portal password.
  7. Press any key to close the black window. The site stays online.
The address changes every time you turn the site on, so always copy the new one.

Turn it off

  1. Open F:\Gitrepo\vigil mdr\tools.
  2. Double-click site-off.cmd.
  3. When it shows Vigil is OFFLINE, press any key.

Turn the site off when you finish, because it holds real hostnames, usernames, IP addresses and command lines.

What keeps running

The background job keeps collecting and analyzing detections while the site is off, as long as you're logged in. New incidents will be waiting when you turn the site back on.

If it doesn't work

ProblemWhat to do
The tunnel did not startCheck your internet connection, then double-click site-on.cmd again
The address shows an errorWait 30 seconds and refresh. If it continues, run site-off.cmd, then site-on.cmd
Wrong passwordCheck Caps Lock, or set a new password using the steps below
Too many attemptsWait 15 minutes, or run site-off.cmd, then site-on.cmd
Database offlineLog out and back in to Windows, or run Start-ScheduledTask -TaskName 'Vigil PostgreSQL'
Ollama offlineStart Ollama. New incidents wait until it's running

Forgot your password?

Your password can't be recovered, but you can set a new one. Open PowerShell and run:

cd "F:\Gitrepo\vigil mdr"
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\set-portal-password.ps1

Type a new password of at least 14 characters, twice. Nothing appears while you type. Then close that window, and double-click site-off.cmd followed by site-on.cmd.

Keep it safe

  • Don't share your password or the live address with people who shouldn't see your data.
  • Turn the site off when you aren't using it.
  • Your laptop is the server, so the site stops if it sleeps or shuts down.
